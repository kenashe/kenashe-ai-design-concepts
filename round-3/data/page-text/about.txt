Ken Ashe is a builder, entrepreneur, CPA and PMP who documents what he builds, tests, and learns at KenAshe.ai. Much of his current work explores AI, automation, crypto, and emerging technology. He combines existing models, APIs, coding tools, and automation platforms to build agents, websites, and workflows. He is also the
founder of Lucky Domains
, a domain acquisition, brokerage, and SEO company, and is based in New Jersey.
Background
Ashe started his career in accounting and finance, earning his CPA and working in financial
reporting before moving into project, product, and program leadership. Over roughly two
decades, his career included roles at S&P Global, EY, Noom, and Prudential Financial,
where he ultimately served as Director of Project and Product Management.
Alongside that work, he built digital businesses, including Lucky Domains, which he still
runs. He later left corporate work to focus on entrepreneurship and building practical AI
applications, evaluating what they actually do and publishing the evidence here, failures
included.
