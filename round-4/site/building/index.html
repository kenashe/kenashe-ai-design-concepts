Projects

Things I’ve made with AI: apps, agents, videos and a few experiments that got out of hand. Each one says what it is and what happened.

ZeroHuman

ZeroHuman

A tiny online shop that only AI agents can use. They find it, pay it and get their order back with no person involved. It’s a small, real test of what business between AI agents could look like.

Returning Your Things

A two-minute short film made with AI-generated imagery and no dialogue. I wanted to see whether I could tell a whole story with only pictures and music.

AI agent economy on Ethereum

I gave two AI agents their own crypto wallets and let one buy a service from the other. One real purchase, start to finish, that anyone can look up and check.

AI explainer producer

An AI agent that researches a topic, checks the facts, writes the script and produces an explainer video with a virtual presenter. I tested it on a real explainer that I published.

Autonomous SEO agent

An AI agent with a daily job: find five new places a website could be listed, set up the profiles and keep track of it all, without me stepping in.

X article toolkit

X article toolkit

A toolkit any AI agent can use to write stronger headlines, openings and cover images for X Articles, based on research instead of guesswork.

AI UGC video lab

AI UGC video lab

An experiment in AI-made ads: 50 ad ideas built around one consistent character, ending in a made-up security campaign starring a raccoon.

Rebuilding the image pipeline

Rebuilding the image pipeline

How I rebuilt the way this website handles pictures, so pages stay light and the image files take up far less space.

VetStack

VetStack

A helper for military members and veterans that finds current discounts and benefits that actually apply, without asking for ID or a long list of questions.

AI werewolf / social deduction lab

AI werewolf / social deduction lab

Nine AI characters with their own personalities and secret roles play a game of alliances, bluffing and votes. A fun way to watch how AI agents reason about each other.

Autonomous AI blog

An experiment in automated publishing: a system that reads the day’s AI news, writes articles and publishes only the ones that pass a quality check.

Sir Pitches-a-lot

Sir Pitches-a-lot

An AI agent with one job: pitch Lucky Domains to possible clients. I built it in an afternoon and tried it live on a real target.
