Returning Your Things

A two-minute short film made with AI-generated imagery and no dialogue. I wanted to see whether I could tell a whole story with only pictures and music.

An original short film by Ken Ashe · 2026 · 1:54

Watch the video

On a snowy winter night, a man crosses the city to return his ex’s belongings.

Director’s Note

I made Returning Your Things with AI-generated imagery. I wanted to see if I could tell a whole story without dialogue, using only images and music. The film needed to stand on its own, whether or not you knew how I made it.

Behind the scenes: 4 Tips for Making AI Videos That Feel Like Films. Why I changed the mug instead of the shard, kept the reach but changed his eyes, and chose one piece of music for the whole film.
