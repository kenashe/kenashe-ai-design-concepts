How I got here

I started my career in accounting and finance, earning my CPA and working in financial reporting before moving into project, product and program leadership. Over roughly two decades I worked at S&P Global, EY, Noom and Prudential Financial, where I finished as Director of Project and Product Management.

Alongside that work I built digital businesses, including Lucky Domains, which I still run. I later left corporate work to focus on entrepreneurship and on making, and explaining, practical things with AI.

Lucky Domains is my separate business, a digital presence studio. You can read about that side of my work on the Lucky Domains founder page.
