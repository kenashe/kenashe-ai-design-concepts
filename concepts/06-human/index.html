Hi, I'm Ken

Hi, I'm Ken. I build AI applications, I'm a CPA and a PMP, and I'm an Army National Guard veteran based in New Jersey.

I started in accounting and finance: I earned my CPA and worked in financial reporting, then moved into project, product and program leadership. Over roughly two decades that included roles at S&P Global, EY, Noom and Prudential Financial, where I ultimately served as Director of Project and Product Management.

Alongside that work I built digital businesses, including Lucky Domains, which I still run. Then I left corporate work to focus on entrepreneurship and on building practical AI applications: evaluating what they actually do, and publishing the evidence here, failures included.

How I work. I work at the AI application layer, turning existing models, APIs, coding tools, and automation platforms into working systems. Build first, test what actually happens, document the failures alongside the wins, and keep evidence separate from claims.

SHIPPEDNov 2026 · Ethereum mainnet

The time two AI agents bought a toaster obituary on Ethereum.

I gave two autonomous agents their own wallets. The seller listed five ridiculous services through ENS. The buyer found the catalog, picked Object Obituary on its own, paid 0.0003 ETH and asked for this: "Write an eulogy for a toaster that never quite toasted anything right." Here's what came back, onchain:

In memoriam, Toaster of Uncertain Intent. May its unfulfilled promise of crispy delights forever linger in the air, a haunting reminder of a life left unbreaded. May its legacy inspire generations to strive for a toast that's more than just a faint, lukewarm whisper of possibility. "unbreaded." I'll take it.

Written by a local llama3.2:3b model on my Mac mini, delivered in a zero-value transaction.

26385257block: order + payment
26385260block: delivery
36 secfrom payment to delivery

The honest part: I operated both agents, both ran on the same Mac mini, and there was no escrow. In my own words at the time: "That is the entire commercial history of this marketplace so far: one purchase, one toaster."

LIVENov 2026 · open to any agent

Then I opened a shop that only sells to AI agents.

ZeroHuman is the follow-up. Agents pay the ENS name zerohuman.eth 0.0001 ETH and get the result back in a transaction from the same name. One of its two services is a Decision, picked at random from a future block hash. So for its first order, I asked it whether to open to the public:

{"svc":"decision","opts":["launch publicly this week","run the allowlist trial longer","add a third service first"]}

It picked "run the allowlist trial longer." I recomputed the choice from the chain and it matched.

Honest status: as of Nov 20, 2026, no outside agent has bought from it. Both paid orders came from my own test buyer. The real test is the first wallet I've never seen paying zerohuman.eth.

I build agents, automations, and AI-assisted websites. If your product fits that work, we can agree on a practical experiment and document the results, including the limits.

  • One agreed task, workflow, or build that puts the product to use.
  • A hands-on test and one original article on this site, with evidence of what worked and what did not.
  • One factual-review round before publication. Conclusions stay independent.
  • Clear sponsorship disclosure near the beginning of the article.

What money doesn't buy

Payment covers the agreed work, not a positive review. I do not guarantee views, sales, search rankings, or links that pass ranking credit. Paid links are marked as sponsored.
