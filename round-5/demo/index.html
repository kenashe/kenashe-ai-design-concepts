Updating the site, demonstrated

Six ordinary updates, made one after another in an isolated copy of the real production files. After each one the records were re-read, the site was rebuilt and the result was compared with the build before it, so the lists below are measured, not described.

  • Where the edits were made: a local copy of the production repository at 8b35b45 with the proposed content fields applied, on a throwaway branch with no remote. The first three steps edit src/data/builds.ts, src/data/writing.ts and page files there. Each is saved as a patch you can read.
  • What was not possible: Astro could not be installed in my environment, so production’s own templates were not run. The records are read by importing the real TypeScript files; the two example write-ups are read from their page files by a small reader. The pages you see are the prototype’s.
  • Kept apart: the example entries exist only in this demonstration build. The review prototype contains none of them, and nothing was pushed to production.
